## 研究更新（已建笔记）

- **[The Effectiveness and Durability of Pretraining Data Filtering in Protein Language Models](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/research/the-effectiveness-and-durability-of-pretraining-data-filtering-in-protein-language-models)** · UK AISI · AISI Research — TL;DR：在 8M–3B 的 ESM-2 蛋白质模型上，从预训练数据中过滤病毒蛋白能选择性削弱相关能力，规模越大效果越明显；但用约 80 美元算力 fine-tune 就能完全恢复，所以单靠过滤不是稳健的 safeguard。→ [[papers/UK AISI/2026-10-26 The Effectiveness and Durability of Pretraining Data Filtering in Protein Language Models|笔记]]
- **[ReproBAIT: Can coding agents build their own biological AI tools?](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/research/reprobait-can-coding-agents-build-their-own-biological-ai-tools)** · UK AISI · AISI Research — TL;DR：新评估测 coding agent 能否根据有限的公开信息重建生物 AI 工具；较简单的工具只花几到二十美元就能复现到接近原版，最复杂的没能复现。说明只扣留代码不等于安全发布，数据访问控制更关键。→ [[papers/UK AISI/2026-10-26 ReproBAIT Can coding agents build their own biological AI tools|笔记]]
